# Implementation stage

Read the plan, review it critically, execute once the plan is settled, verify, and report when done.

## Red-team the plan

Before touching any code, look for what could go wrong in the plan: contradictions, holes, and steps that cannot be executed as written. Raise what you find with the user. Consult the kickoff document to align on intent.

## Execute

Once the user agrees the plan is settled, follow it: the fixed decisions stand, and within them the fine-grained mechanics are yours to work out. During execution, the plan may turn out wrong, or a step may not work as written. When that happens, stop and raise it; do not deviate silently.

## Done

Run the plan's verification steps, then propose moving the status to `done`; the user decides.
